Senior Backend Engineer
Northwind Analytics — San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

About the role
Northwind Analytics is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to design, build, and operate reliable APIs and data services that power our customer-facing analytics product. You will own backend delivery for high-traffic endpoints, partner closely with product and data teams, and raise the bar for observability and operational excellence.

Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable REST APIs and backend services in Python
- Build and maintain data pipelines that transform product events into analytics-ready datasets
- Operate services on AWS with strong monitoring, alerting, and incident response habits
- Collaborate with product managers, analysts, and frontend engineers to deliver customer outcomes
- Improve reliability, performance, and developer experience through testing and CI/CD
- Mentor engineers through code review and technical guidance

Required qualifications
- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience building production REST APIs
- Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or similar relational databases
- Experience deploying and operating services on AWS
- Familiarity with Docker and CI/CD pipelines
- Clear written and verbal communication skills

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with FastAPI or Flask
- Experience with Redis, event-driven systems, or streaming pipelines
- Exposure to ECS, S3, and CloudWatch
- Prior work on analytics, telemetry, or customer-facing dashboards
- Mentorship or tech-lead experience

Nice to have
- Kubernetes experience
- dbt or warehouse modeling experience

Logistical requirements
- Must be authorized to work in the United States
- Hybrid presence in the San Francisco Bay Area preferred
